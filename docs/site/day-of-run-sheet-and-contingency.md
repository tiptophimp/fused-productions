# FUSED PRODUCTIONS - DAY-OF RUN-SHEET & CONTINGENCY PLAYBOOK

## 1. Centralized Run-Sheet & Cue-to-Cue Structure

Every event deployment operates on a synchronized timeline accessible in real-time across all crew leads and sub-vendors via mobile devices.

- **Load-In & Staging Window:** Strict arrival windows for rental drop-offs, mobile staging, and tent construction.
- **Sound Check & Technical Alignment:** Timed audio engineering checks, lighting programming runs, and culinary prep milestones.
- **Event Execution (Cue-to-Cue):** Minute-by-minute tracking of ceremony/gala highlights, speeches, and entertainment blocks.

## 2. Redundancy & Emergency Contingency Playbooks

Professional execution requires rigorous preparation for unexpected disruptions:

- **Redundant Technical Infrastructure:** Backup power generators, secondary audio mixing rigs, and redundant cabling staged on-site for every production.
- **Weather & Emergency Protocols:** Standardized indoor fallback plans, tent climate control contingency matrices, and emergency medical response guidelines.
- **Post-Event Review & Debrief:** Automated client satisfaction survey triggered in the portal 48 hours post-event, paired with an internal crew debrief to refine future operational efficiency.
