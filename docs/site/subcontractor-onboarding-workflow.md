# FUSED PRODUCTIONS - SUBCONTRACTOR ONBOARDING WORKFLOW

## 1. Automated Onboarding Pipeline

To maintain strict quality control and eliminate multi-vendor chaos, all external partners must clear the automated digital onboarding pipeline before receiving event assignments.

## 2. Step-by-Step Onboarding Process

1. **Invitation & Portal Registration:** Subcontractor receives a secure registration link to access the Fused Productions Vendor Portal.
2. **Compliance Documentation Upload:**
   - Submission of a verified W-9 form.
   - Upload of a current Certificate of Insurance (COI) explicitly naming Fused Productions as an additional insured.
3. **SLA Execution:** Digital signature execution of the Master Subcontractor Agreement and Service Level Agreement (SLA) covering presentation standards, conduct, and punctuality.
4. **Activation & Dispatch Clearance:** Once compliance flags clear, the admin approves the profile, unlocking access to the gig dispatch board and synchronized run-sheets.
