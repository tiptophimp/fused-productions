# FUSED PRODUCTIONS - PUBLIC MARKETING SITE SPECIFICATION

## 1. Frontend Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (React) for optimized server-side rendering (SSR) and fast initial page loads.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with a high-contrast dark theme, metallic gold accents, and sharp typography.

## 2. Page Components & Layouts

- **Hero Section:** Full-width background video loop of high-end corporate events and weddings, featuring the core value proposition: *All-in-One Event Production & Management under a Single Contract*.
- **Value Proposition Grid:** Interactive cards highlighting the elimination of multi-vendor chaos, fixed pricing structures, and dedicated client portals.
- **Inquiry & Conversion Funnel:** A multi-step lead capture form requesting event type, guest count, estimated budget, and preferred dates to route prospects directly into the CRM pipeline.
