# FUSED PRODUCTIONS - MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT (MSA)

## 1. Parties & Scope of Agreement

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **Fused Productions** ("General Contractor") and the undersigned Client ("Client"). This Agreement governs the comprehensive production, management, and execution of all event services detailed in the attached Statement of Work (SOW).

## 2. Single Point of Accountability

Fused Productions acts as the sole general contractor for the event. Fused Productions assumes full operational liability and direct management responsibility for all sub-vendors, rentals, culinary services, bar operations, and entertainment integration. The Client shall maintain a single point of contact through Fused Productions for all pre-planning, logistics, and day-of execution.

## 3. Fixed Pricing & Statement of Work

All services, equipment rentals, labor, and management fees are consolidated into a single locked-in total cost specified in the attached SOW. This structure entirely eliminates hidden service charges, unexpected vendor invoices, or unapproved add-on fees.

## 4. Milestone-Based Billing Schedule

Payments shall be executed strictly according to the following milestone schedule via the Fused Productions Client Portal:

- **Initial Retainer (30%):** Due immediately upon contract execution and client portal provisioning, securing the event date.
- **Planning Phase Draw (40%):** Due exactly 60 days prior to the event date, triggered upon final design, layout, and menu approval.
- **Final Settlement (30%):** Due 14 days prior to the event date, adjusted and finalized based on final guest count numbers and any approved change orders.

## 5. Cancellation & Force Majeure

In the event of cancellation or postponement, retainer and milestone draws are governed by the sliding scale outlined in the portal terms. Force Majeure events shall be managed via standardized rescheduling protocols prioritizing mutual mitigation.
