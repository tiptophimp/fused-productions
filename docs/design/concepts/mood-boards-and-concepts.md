# Fused Productions - Concept & Mood Board Guidelines

## Purpose
This folder houses mood boards, style decks, and visual mockups presented to clients during the design approval phase.

## Standard Concept Categories
1. **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Stage washes, uplighting layouts, and immersive design concepts.
2. **Tablescapes & Infrastructure:** Tent setups, custom staging modules, and linen/decor combinations.
3. **Branding & Signage:** Corporate gala branding layout samples and custom entry signage mockups.
